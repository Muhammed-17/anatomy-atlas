3D Anatomy Atlas

Interactive 3D model of the adult human body

Female model: real Visible Human Female reference data from HuBMAP: skin, breasts with milk ducts, nipple and ligaments, uterus, cervix, vagina, ovaries, uterine tubes, bladder, kidneys, heart, lungs, liver, pancreas, intestines, brain, larynx, tonsils, vessels, spinal cord, pelvis and knees. Click an organ for its location and function. The stomach, thyroid, parathyroid, adrenal and pituitary glands are borrowed from the male model and fitted to this body, so their size and position are approximate. The placenta is a full-term specimen shown at real size in front of the body, because it was scanned separately from the non-pregnant uterus. Not available: muscles, joints and most of the skeleton.
